# RazorpayX Payroll Business Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `razorpayx_payroll`
- **Billing Type:** Automated Enterprise Payroll & Statutory Compliance

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Emergency Salary Grace Period:** Mandatory **5-day emergency grace period** for salary disbursement accounts to ensure employees receive timely payouts while corporate debit mandates clear.
2. **Zero Interest Fee:** No late charges applied during 5-day grace window.
3. **Escalation Trigger:** Direct escalation to Razorpay Risk team if mandate remains un-funded on Day 6.
