# Slack Workspace Subscription Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `slack_workspace`
- **Billing Type:** Pro & Business+ Workspace Subscriptions

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Fair-Billing Inactive User Credit:** Before generating payment link, agent checks for inactive user seat credits. Any unapplied active credits are deducted from overdue amount automatically.
2. **Grace Extension:** **5-day grace buffer** granted to active workspace admins facing bank authorization failures.
3. **Discount Policy:** Max 3% volume discount for workspaces with > 50 seats.
