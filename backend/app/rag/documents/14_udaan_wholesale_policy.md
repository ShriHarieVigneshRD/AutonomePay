# Udaan Wholesale B2B Settlement Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `udaan_wholesale`
- **Billing Type:** Bulk Logistics & Wholesale Goods Credit

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Two-Stage Milestone Settlement:** For bulk wholesale orders exceeding **INR 50,000**, agent is authorized to create a 2-stage milestone payment link structure: **40% immediate initial payment** and **60% balance payable on Day 15**.
2. **Grace Extension:** 5 days grace period for verified retail buyers.
3. **Discount Ceiling:** Max 2% concession on full single-tranche settlement.
