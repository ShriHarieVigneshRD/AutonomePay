# QuickKart B2B Settlement Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `quickkart_b2b`
- **Billing Type:** B2B Merchant Supply Invoices (Net-30)

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Disputed Portion Hold & Split Link:** If customer disputes order fulfillment or damaged inventory items (up to 20% of invoice), agent holds disputed portion and generates immediate Razorpay payment link for **80% undisputed balance**.
2. **Prompt Payment Discount:** Early settlement within 24 hours of dispute resolution earns a **3% prompt payment discount**.
3. **Grace Period:** Net-30 invoice terms with 7 days grace.
