# JioFiber Broadband Settlement Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `jio_fiber`
- **Billing Type:** Broadband & Fiber Subscription

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Emergency Data Top-Up:** If bank server down or UPI mandate failed due to NPCI issues, agent may issue a **48-hour emergency connectivity link** granting full speed access while retry link is processed.
2. **Grace Extension:** Maximum **3-day grace period** for monthly postpaid broadband invoices.
3. **Discount Policy:** No direct invoice discounts. Fee waivers apply only for verified gateway outage failures.
