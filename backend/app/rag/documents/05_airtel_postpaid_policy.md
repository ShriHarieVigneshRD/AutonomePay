# Airtel Postpaid Settlement Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `airtel_postpaid`
- **Billing Type:** Postpaid Telecom & Mobility

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Late Fee Waiver:** Standard late payment fee of **INR 100** is completely waived if customer completes settlement within 24 hours of bot contact via instant Razorpay UPI link.
2. **Safe-Custody Pause:** Customers traveling or facing temporary financial constraints can request a Safe-Custody pause for up to **60 days** with a nominal fee of INR 50/month.
3. **Grace Window:** 3 days grace before outgoing call bar; 7 days before complete connection suspension.
