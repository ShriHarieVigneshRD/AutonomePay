# Amazon Prime India Settlement & Subscription Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `amazon_prime`
- **Supported Plans:**
  - Prime Monthly: INR 299 / month
  - Prime Annual: INR 1499 / year
  - Prime Lite: INR 799 / year

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Annual Grace Extension:** For failed Annual Prime renewals (INR 1499), a maximum **5-day grace period** is permitted during which Prime Video and Shipping benefits remain active.
2. **Monthly Transition:** Customers unable to commit to INR 1499 annual payment may be transitioned to the INR 299 Monthly plan.
3. **Discount Ceiling:** Maximum discount allowed on annual plan retry is **5%** (INR 75 max).
