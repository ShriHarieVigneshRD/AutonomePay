# Zoho One Enterprise Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `zoho_one`
- **Billing Type:** All-in-one SaaS Suite

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Contract Extension:** **10-day payment extension** permitted for annual contract renewals.
2. **Concession Ceiling:** Maximum concession/discount permitted is **3%** of total contract value.
3. **Grace Window:** 10 days grace before domain portal restriction.
