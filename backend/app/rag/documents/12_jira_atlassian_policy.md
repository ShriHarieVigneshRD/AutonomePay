# Jira Atlassian Corporate Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `jira_atlassian`
- **Billing Type:** Jira Cloud & Confluence Enterprise

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Corporate Invoice Extension:** Verified corporate entities (GST registered) can request a **14-day formal invoice extension** without service interruption.
2. **Discounts:** Zero discounts on standard cloud tiers.
3. **Grace Window:** 14 calendar days grace period.
