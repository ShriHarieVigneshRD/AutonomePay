# Swiggy One Membership Settlement Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `swiggy_one`
- **Supported Plans:**
  - Swiggy One Quarterly: INR 299
  - Swiggy One Annual: INR 899

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **No Split Payments:** Split payments are strictly forbidden for consumer membership plans.
2. **High-LTV Concession:** High tenure/LTV users (tenure > 6 months) renewal failure can receive a **10% coupon concession (max INR 30)** applied directly to the Razorpay payment link.
3. **Grace Period:** 48 hours buffer before Swiggy One free delivery privileges are suspended.
