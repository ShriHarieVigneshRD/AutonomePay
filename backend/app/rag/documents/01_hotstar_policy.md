# Disney+ Hotstar Settlement & Subscription Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `hotstar`
- **Supported Plans:**
  - Mobile Plan: INR 149 / quarter
  - Super Plan: INR 299 / quarter
  - Premium Plan: INR 499 / quarter or INR 1499 / year

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Renewal Concessions:** Autonomous agents may grant a maximum renewal discount of **INR 20** for Super or Premium tier renewals if the user cites financial constraints.
2. **Plan Downgrades:** If a customer cannot pay for Premium or Super plans, the agent is authorized to offer an immediate downgrade to the Mobile Plan (INR 149) with immediate Payment Link issuance.
3. **Subscription Pause:** Customers experiencing temporary cash flow issues can pause their active subscription for up to **30 days** without losing watch history or profile data.
4. **Grace Period:** Standard grace period for failed auto-debits is **3 days**.
