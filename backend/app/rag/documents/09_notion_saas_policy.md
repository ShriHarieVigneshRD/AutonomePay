# Notion SaaS Workspace Settlement Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `notion_saas`
- **Supported Plans:**
  - Plus Plan: INR 800 / seat / month
  - Business Plan: INR 1500 / seat / month
  - Enterprise: Custom billing

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Milestone Split Settlement:** For enterprise & corporate invoices over **INR 10,000**, agent is authorized to structure a **50/50 split milestone payment**: 50% payable immediately via Razorpay link, and remaining 50% scheduled 14 days later.
2. **Per-Seat Discount Ceiling:** Zero discount permitted on individual per-seat pricing.
3. **Grace Period:** Standard **7-day grace window** before workspace converts to view-only mode.
