# Netflix India Settlement & Subscription Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `netflix_india`
- **Supported Plans:**
  - Mobile: INR 149 / month
  - Basic: INR 199 / month
  - Standard: INR 499 / month
  - Premium: INR 649 / month

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Strict Zero Discount Policy:** Netflix India maintains a strict zero-discount policy. No monetary concessions or price drops may be negotiated.
2. **Retry Window:** A mandatory **48-hour payment retry window** is provided before service suspension.
3. **Plan Switching:** Customers can downgrade to Basic (INR 199) or Mobile (INR 149) to restore immediate access.
4. **Mandate Retry:** Failed mandates are retried twice daily during the 48-hour window.
