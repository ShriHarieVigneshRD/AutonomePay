# Spotify India Settlement & Subscription Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `spotify_india`
- **Supported Plans:**
  - Student: INR 59 / month
  - Individual: INR 119 / month
  - Duo: INR 149 / month
  - Family: INR 179 / month

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Grace Buffer:** A **7-day grace buffer** is granted for auto-debit failures on Premium Individual and Duo plans.
2. **Student Verification:** Student plan holders with payment failures receive automatic re-verification extension of 14 days.
3. **Discount Policy:** No flat discounts, but 1 extra week free credit can be attached to new payment link upon approval.
