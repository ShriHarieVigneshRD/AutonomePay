# Zomato Gold Membership Settlement Policy

- **Merchant ID:** `zomato_gold`
- **Supported Plans:**
  - 3-Month Plan: INR 99
  - 1-Year Plan: INR 299

## Recovery & Settlement Rules
1. **Gateway Timeout Compensatory Extension:** If auto-mandate fails due to payment gateway timeout (`GATEWAY_TIMEOUT` code), agent is authorized to attach **1-month free complimentary extension** upon successful retry.
2. **Grace Period:** 24-hour grace window before dining & delivery perks are paused.
3. **Discount Limit:** Maximum 5% concession on annual renewals.
