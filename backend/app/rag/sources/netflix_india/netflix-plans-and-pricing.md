# Plans and Pricing

Netflix plans are billed monthly on the date you signed up. We do not offer annual, multi-month, or lifetime membership plans.


Sign up for Netflix today and choose from several payment options. You can easily change your plan or cancel at any time.

You cannot share your Netflix account outside of your household, which is a collection of devices connected to the internet at the main residence where you watch Netflix. People who are not in your household can be added as an extra member.

| Netflix Plans | Features | 
| Mobile | Unlimited ad-free movies, TV shows, and games Watch on 1 phone or tablet at a time Watch in 480p (SD) Download on 1 phone or tablet at a time | 
| Basic | Unlimited ad-free movies, TV shows, and games Watch on 1 supported device at a time Watch in 720p (HD) Download on 1 supported device at a time | 
| Standard | Unlimited ad-free movies, TV shows, and games Watch on 2 supported devices at a time Watch in 1080p (Full HD) Download on 2 supported devices at a time | 
| Premium | Unlimited ad-free movies, TV shows, and games Watch on 4 supported devices at a time Watch in 4K (Ultra HD) + HDR Download on 6 supported devices at a time Netflix spatial audio | 

Note: The Mobile Plan requires a mobile device with Android OS 7 or later, Fire OS 6 or later, or iOS/iPadOS 12 or later. Casting or mirroring from a mobile device is not supported on this plan.

### Pricing (Indian Rupee)

- **Mobile:** ₹149 INR/month
- **Basic**: ₹199 INR/month
- **Standard**: ₹499 INR/month
- **Premium**: ₹649 INR/month

Depending on where you live, you may be charged taxes in addition to your membership price.

Plan prices and extra member eligibility may vary if you have Netflix through a package or add-on.
