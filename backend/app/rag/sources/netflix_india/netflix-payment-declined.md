# Netflix Billing and Payment Help

Netflix membership plans are billed monthly on the date you signed up. We do not offer annual, multi-month, or lifetime membership plans.


If you pay for Netflix through a third party (e.g., your phone or internet provider), you may need to contact your provider for specific billing information or to address issues.
